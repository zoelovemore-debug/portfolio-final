Hands-on Cybersecurity Labs

Five connected labs (75 steps) completed at Forvis Mazars in a simulated environment for a fictional company, CyberGuard Solutions, using a Kali Linux workstation and a Metasploitable2 practice server.

17 steps

Lab 1: Defence in Depth

Installed ClamAV, set up UFW with default-deny and an SSH rule limited to the lab network, listed pending patches and gave MySQL users only the access they needed. Found MySQL 5.0 running with a blank root password.

13 steps

Lab 2: SIEM, Scanning and Backups

Automated patching, deployed a Wazuh agent, triaged alerts at level 10 and above, reviewed file changes, ran a Greenbone scan and restored a database backup to prove it worked.

11 steps

Lab 3: Baselines and Anomaly Detection

Built baselines for logins, processes and connections with grep, awk, ps and netstat. Flagged three real anomalies, rejected two false alarms and traced 38 failed SSH logins to the one address that then got in.

24 steps

Lab 4: Incident Response and Recovery

Blocked a reverse shell with a targeted iptables rule, hashed and quarantined the malware, and removed the persistence entry, rogue account and web shell. Hardened SSH, verified the clean-up, restored data from a pre-intrusion backup and assessed POPIA and 24-hour notification impact.

10 steps

Lab 5: Disaster Recovery and Post-Incident Review

Set RPO and RTO targets for five business functions, found recovery gaps, and ran a blameless review that traced the attack to weak credentials and open password login. Wrote testable improvements using NIST, CIS and FIDO2.
